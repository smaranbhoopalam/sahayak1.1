# 🩺 Sahayak

> **AI-powered post-discharge recovery platform with a Living Digital Twin for continuous patient monitoring and recovery intelligence.**

## 📖 Overview

Sahayak is an AI-driven healthcare platform designed to bridge the gap between hospital discharge and full recovery. Instead of relying solely on periodic follow-ups, Sahayak creates a **Living Digital Twin** for every patient that continuously reflects their recovery status through daily updates, AI-powered analysis, and clinician oversight.

The platform enables patients, doctors, and hospitals to collaborate throughout the recovery journey, reducing the risk of complications, improving adherence, and helping prevent avoidable readmissions.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Patients often receive limited monitoring after being discharged from the hospital. Missed medications, worsening symptoms, poor adherence, and delayed interventions can result in complications and preventable hospital readmissions.

Traditional follow-up methods are:
- Reactive instead of proactive
- Dependent on scheduled appointments
- Difficult for doctors to continuously monitor
- Inaccessible for patients with limited internet connectivity

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Sahayak introduces a **Living Digital Twin** that serves as a real-time digital representation of a patient's recovery.

The Digital Twin continuously evolves using:
- Daily patient check-ins
- AI recovery analysis
- Recovery protocols
- Doctor interventions
- SMS updates
- Automated IVR phone calls

This enables early detection of recovery deviations while providing doctors with explainable clinical insights.

# ✨ Key Features

## 🧍 Living Digital Twin

- Personalised Digital Twin generated after patient onboarding
- Colour-coded recovery status
- Interactive body map
- Daily evolution based on patient recovery

---

## 📅 Recovery Timeline

- Interactive timeline slider
- View recovery progress day-by-day
- Compare historical recovery states
- Review milestones and interventions

---

## 📲 Multiple Recovery Update Methods

Patients can update their recovery through:

- Mobile application
- SMS
- Automated IVR phone calls

This ensures monitoring continues even without internet access.

---

## 🤖 AI Recovery Intelligence

The AI engine continuously analyses recovery using:

- Recovery Confidence Score (RCS)
- Recovery Drift Index (RDI)
- Symptom progression
- Medication adherence
- Recovery barriers

---

## 👨‍⚕️ Doctor Workspace

Doctors can:

- Monitor assigned patients
- View Digital Twins
- Review AI insights
- Receive alerts
- Send interventions
- Track recovery trends

---

## 🏥 Hospital Command Centre

Hospitals can monitor:

- Active recoveries
- High-risk patients
- Readmission risks
- Recovery analytics
- Population-level insights

---

## 🔔 Smart Recovery Reminders

If a patient misses a daily update:

1. Push Notification
2. SMS Reminder
3. Automated Phone Call
4. Doctor Alert (if unanswered)

---

## 📈 Explainable AI

Instead of only generating risk scores, Sahayak explains why recovery has deviated, allowing clinicians to make informed decisions.
